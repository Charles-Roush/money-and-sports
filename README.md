# Does money make a sports team win?

An analysis of 40 seasons of MLB payroll and win data.

## Findings

- **Spending and winning are positively correlated in 37 of 40 seasons.** The yearly correlation averages about 0.40 and ranges from −0.22 to 0.86.
- **Money mattered less in the past.**
- **The payroll gap is widening.**
- **Some teams buy wins more reliably than others,** like the Dodgers (*r* = 0.62), Brewers (0.57), and Marlins (0.52).

## Implementation notes

The statistics and plotting code are split into two small reusable libraries: `stats_lib.py` and `plot_lib.py`.
